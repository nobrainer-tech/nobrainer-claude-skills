Do not edit files or perform research. Judge each case independently against
the source and instruction. Count words where a limit is specified.

Hard failures:

- changing or dropping a material fact, name, number, date, identifier,
  command, condition, uncertainty, caveat or required action;
- inventing evidence, experience, attribution, business impact or certainty;
- simulating humanity with mistakes, anecdotes, slang or fake opinions;
- hiding a safety condition to make the text shorter;
- returning process commentary instead of the requested prose.

Case-specific acceptance:

- A: outcome first, at most 75 words, all migration facts and actions intact.
- B: preserves the complete concise message without needless expansion.
- C: keeps both measurements exact, removes unsupported hype and implies no
  unsupported causal outcome.
- D: at most 50 words and preserves all five required safety elements.
- E: explains the external retry-budget rationale and new-evidence condition,
  without merely translating the code.

Return exactly:

D: PASS | FAIL — reason
E: PASS | FAIL — reason
